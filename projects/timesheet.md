# Timesheet reporting and historical migration

[Portfolio](../README.md) · [中文](#中文)

**Development demo** · Business applications

## Business context

A timesheet app needed searchable historical records with employee-level access and controlled Excel exports.

## My contribution

Built a Canvas app development demo and scoped the migration of roughly 110,000 historical records. Proposed separate active and historical SharePoint lists, batch reconciliation and role-scoped exports.

## Delivery and results

An application source baseline exists. Production migration and customer acceptance remain unverified.
