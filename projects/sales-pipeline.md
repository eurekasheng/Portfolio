# Global sales pipeline reporting

[Portfolio](../README.md) · [中文](#中文)

**Delivered** · Analytics

## Business context

Regional sales teams and management needed a common view of targets and open pipeline.

## My contribution

Owned delivery from SQL data preparation through reusable Power BI modeling, DAX, row-level security and report publication.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[SQL data preparation] --> B[Power BI semantic model]
    B --> C[Measures and access rules]
    C --> D[Regional reporting]
```

## Delivery and results

The reporting platform covered more than 100,000 pipeline records for an audience of roughly 400 managers and business users, per project records.
