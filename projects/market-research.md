# Manufacturing market discovery for Power Platform services

## Business context

An independent consulting practice needed a repeatable way to identify where training and automation services might be valuable.

## My contribution

Built a scoring model using public job descriptions, Microsoft adoption signals and local capacity evidence. Gave weight to Power BI listed as a desirable skill in operational roles.

## Delivery and results

The framework and research template are in place. Prospect validation and sales outcomes are pending.
