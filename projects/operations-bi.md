# Shared services reporting

[Portfolio](../README.md) · [中文](#中文)

## Business context

Shared services leaders needed visibility into operating risk, staffing, orders and spend.

## My contribution

Developed and maintained Power BI reporting for procurement, order-to-cash, HR and related operations. Co-delivered an embedded global project report with colleagues in Poland.

## Delivery and results

The reporting suite included continuity risk, attendance, meeting costs and quarterly order trends.
